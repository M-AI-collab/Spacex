# Part 3: a realistic path to $30,000 a year of trading profit

Annual profit = capital x return, so **$30k/year needs capital of $30,000 / return**:

| Expected return | Capital needed for $30k/yr profit |
|---|---|
| 10%/yr (stock index long-run, or bot in a weak period) | $300,000 (AED 1,101,750) |
| 15%/yr (bot, wide 10-coin universe, out-of-sample) | $200,000 (AED 734,500) |
| 25%/yr (bot, mid-case of out-of-sample results) | $120,000 (AED 440,700) |
| 35%/yr (bot, optimistic: matches best out-of-sample run) | $85,714 (AED 314,786) |

## Time to reach it from $1,000 + monthly contributions

Years until the account is big enough to make $30k/yr (total you put in, in brackets):

| Monthly contribution | 10%/yr | 15%/yr | 25%/yr | 35%/yr |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $250/month (AED 918) | 24.4 yrs ($74,250) | 16.4 yrs ($50,250) | 10.0 yrs ($31,000) | 7.2 yrs ($22,750) |
| $500/month (AED 1,836) | 18.2 yrs ($110,500) | 12.3 yrs ($75,000) | 7.5 yrs ($46,000) | 5.5 yrs ($34,000) |
| $1,000/month (AED 3,672) | 12.8 yrs ($154,000) | 8.6 yrs ($104,000) | 5.2 yrs ($64,000) | 3.8 yrs ($47,000) |
| $2,000/month (AED 7,345) | 8.2 yrs ($199,000) | 5.6 yrs ($135,000) | 3.4 yrs ($83,000) | 2.4 yrs ($59,000) |
| $3,000/month (AED 11,018) | 6.2 yrs ($223,000) | 4.2 yrs ($151,000) | 2.5 yrs ($91,000) | 1.8 yrs ($67,000) |

Assumes returns are earned smoothly every year. Real returns arrive in lumps, with -40% to -60% drawdowns along the way (see Part 1), so treat these as averages, not promises.

# SpaceX (Nasdaq: SPCX): Equity Research Note

**Date:** Oct 29, 2026 · **Price:** $145.23 (Oct 28 close; ~$142.6 pre-market Oct 29) · **Rating: HOLD** · **Confidence: medium-low**

> **Disclosure:** I'm Claude, built by Anthropic. Anthropic is widely reported to be SpaceX's largest customer (34.5% of Q3 revenue). It also competes with xAI's Grok and with Cursor. I count that customer dependency as a risk below, judged on the evidence, so weigh my view with this in mind. This is not personal financial advice.

---

## Status check (verified)

- **It is public.** SpaceX has traded on Nasdaq as **SPCX** since **June 12, 2026**.
  - The IPO priced at **$135**. It raised $75B base, or about $85.7B net including the over-allotment. That makes it the largest IPO ever.
  - It is in the Nasdaq-100. It is **not yet eligible for the S&P 500**, which requires 12 months of trading and four straight quarters of GAAP profit. S&P declined to change its rules for SpaceX.
- **Market cap is about $1.97T** on 13.58B shares. **Enterprise value is about $1.92T**, since the company had roughly $51B of net cash at Sep 30.
- **The 52-week range is $104.83 to $225.64.** The stock fell about 54% from its June 16 peak to its Aug 5 low in seven weeks, then recovered after Q3 results.
- **The company has changed shape.** SpaceX merged with xAI (Grok and X) in Feb 2026 and bought Cursor for $60B in Aug 2026. It now reports three segments, and **AI ("Super Intelligence", or SI) is the largest of them**, ahead of both rockets and Starlink.

**My calculations:**
- EV to 2026E sales is **39x**.
- EV to 2027E sales is **10.8x**, using consensus of about $178B.
- The P/E on 2027E EPS of $2.34 is **62x**.
- If Q1 2027 lands at the midpoint of guidance, EV falls to about 14x annualized sales.

**What the price already assumes:**
- For a 10% annual return over five years, allowing about 1.5% a year of dilution, SpaceX needs a market cap of about $3.4T in 2031.
  - At 25–30x earnings, that means **$115–140B of annual net income**.
  - At a 25% net margin, that means **about $450–550B of revenue**.
  - That is Microsoft- or Alphabet-level profit, and about 10x 2026 revenue.
- Wall Street's consensus already models this path: about $178–181B of revenue in 2027 and about $331B in 2028, the 2028 figure raised from about $249B after Q3.
- **If those numbers arrive, the stock is roughly fairly priced. If they don't, the price has little support beneath it.**
- **The tell:** the market prices pure AI-compute landlords cheaply.
  - CoreWeave trades at about 1.8x forward sales (about 4x on an EV basis, given its debt), and Oracle at about 17x forward earnings.
  - Inside SPCX, a mostly similar business carries a far richer implied multiple. You are paying a Musk premium on the riskiest segment.

## 4. Competitive position

**Moat:**
- **Launch:**
  - The reusable Falcon fleet carries more than 80% of global mass to orbit, with a mission success rate above 99%.
  - Starship reached orbit on Flight 14 in Sept 2026 and deployed 26 V3 Starlink satellites, about 10x the capacity of a Falcon 9 Starlink launch.
- **Starlink:**
  - About 10,000 satellites, versus about 400 for Amazon Leo and about 450 for China's two constellations combined.
  - Launching its own satellites at cost is a structural advantage nobody else has.
  - 3,800 aircraft are equipped, with about 12,000 contracted.
- **AI compute:** SpaceX builds capacity and secures power quickly, but GPUs are a commodity. Its edge is speed, not a durable moat.

| Area | Competitor | Status (Oct 2026) | Threat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy launch | Blue Origin New Glenn | Upper stage underperformed in Apr 2026. A static-fire explosion on May 28 wrecked its only pad, and a return to flight looks likely in 2027. | Low now, rising later |
| Launch | ULA Vulcan | Anomaly in Feb 2026; a GPS mission moved to Falcon 9 | Low |
| Medium launch | Rocket Lab Neutron | Not yet flown. Targets end-2026, likely 2027. | Low |
| Broadband | Amazon Leo (Kuiper) | About 396 of 3,236 satellites in orbit. Consumer service is targeted for late 2026 in 5 countries, and it is behind its FCC milestone. | Medium long-term (Prime bundling, deep pockets) |
| Broadband | China (Guowang, Qianfan) | About 450 satellites combined, state-backed | Medium outside the West |
| Direct-to-phone | AST SpaceMobile + carriers | Bell chose AST. U.S. carriers are lobbying the FCC to limit satellite spectrum. | Medium |

| AI models/apps | OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta; Copilot and Claude Code compete with Cursor | Not covered | High |

## 5. Risks, ranked

1. **AI revenue quality (the biggest risk).**
   - Per the 10-Q, cloud contracts are "typically three years and terminable by either party upon 90 days' notice."
   - Anthropic's non-cancellable periods end in Jan and Mar 2027, and Google can give notice after Dec 31, 2026.
   - Musk prefers pricing close to the spot market.
   - If GPU rental prices fall, as older-generation prices did once supply caught up, revenue reprices within a quarter. The depreciation on $67B of servers and construction-in-progress does not.
2. **Customer concentration.**
   - Customer B (34.5%) and Customer A (16.6%) together made up 51% of Q3 revenue.
   - The thesis partly depends on Anthropic continuing to raise capital. Anthropic filed for its own IPO in Oct 2026.
3. **Capex and financing.**
   - 2026 capex is about $85B, and 2027 will likely be far higher. Free cash flow stays negative for years.
   - Ratings sit at the low end of investment grade, and the debut bonds priced about 40bp wider than BBB peers.
   - Trouble in credit markets would hit the plan directly.
4. **Starship execution.**
   - Flight 15, the first attempt to catch both the booster and the ship, is due in Nov–Dec 2026. A refueling demonstration follows in early 2027.
   - The crewed Moon landing is now Artemis IV, in early 2028.
   - Starlink V3, the NASA lander and orbital data centers all depend on Starship, and Musk's timelines usually slip.
5. **Musk and governance.**
   - **Control:**
     - Musk holds about 85% of the votes, and SpaceX is a "controlled company" without a majority-independent board.
     - Only Class B holders can remove him, which in practice means himself.
     - MSCI's ESG rating is CCC.
   - **Divided attention:** he also runs Tesla and has a Tesla pay package of about $1T, approved in Nov 2025.
   - **Related-party deals:**
     - Valor's equipment leases.
     - Mark-ups of the Boring Co. stake.
     - Tesla Megapack purchases ($480M in 9M).
     - Both boards formed special committees on a Tesla–SpaceX merger, a deal in which Musk sits on both sides.
   - **Political risk:** during their June 2025 feud, the President threatened Musk's federal contracts. The government is about 17% of revenue.
6. **Regulation.**
   - FCC spectrum disputes. The EchoStar deal was approved in May 2026, the 800 MHz Grain deal is pending, and carriers are lobbying against more satellite spectrum.
   - FAA launch licensing, foreign landing rights and export controls.
   - EU rules covering X and Grok.
   - The Louisiana Starbase tax-deal lawsuit, filed Oct 26, is minor.
7. **Supply and dilution.**
   - About 798M shares unlock on Dec 8, 2026. Musk and other large holders unlock in stages from late Jan to Aug 2027; Musk's own lock-up ends June 12, 2027.
   - Stock compensation runs about $1.1B a quarter, plus $23.4B of Cursor awards. The EchoStar deal adds $11.1B of stock.
8. **Valuation.** The stock trades at about 60x trailing sales and has already shown it can lose half its value in weeks.

## 8. Verdict: **HOLD**, not a Strong Buy

**Confidence is medium-low (about 55%), because the range of outcomes is unusually wide.**

**Why not Buy:**
- At about $2T, the price already assumes the consensus, and the consensus assumes Musk's compute plan works. That means revenue up about 10x in three years.
- The marginal growth dollar is GPU rental. It is capital-heavy, cancellable on 90 days' notice, concentrated on one AI lab and priced near cycle highs. Elsewhere, the market values it at 2–4x sales.
- Governance gives shareholders no say.

**Why not Avoid:**
- Starlink is a real compounding monopoly, with 45% margins and 69% growth.
- Launch dominance is real, and the balance sheet is currently strong.
- If the Q1 2027 guide is delivered, the stock gets much cheaper on forward numbers.

**What would change my view:**
1. **Durability of AI contracts.**
   - I would upgrade to Buy if Anthropic and Google convert to long-term, non-cancellable take-or-pay contracts at stable pricing, or if new large customers sign.
   - I would downgrade to Avoid if any major customer gives notice or rental pricing falls.
2. **The 2027 capex guide, due around late Jan 2027.**
   - I would upgrade if spending is matched by contracted revenue and funded without large equity raises.
   - I would downgrade if leverage jumps or the company needs new equity.
3. **Starship full reuse, shown by Flight 15 and the refueling demo.**
   - Success raises the odds of the bull case through V3 Starlink, the Moon lander and orbital compute.
   - Repeated failures lower them.

Price also matters. Below about $110–120, the base case alone offers +33–45%, and the risk/reward becomes favorable.

**What this means for you (20s, 5+ year horizon):**
- Don't make SPCX a core holding at $145.
- If you want exposure, keep it to a small slice of your portfolio, 3–5% at most, and buy in tranches around the known events: the Dec 8 unlock, Q4 results in late Jan and Musk's unlock in June 2027.
- Avoid leverage and options. The stock fell 54% in seven weeks this summer.
- If you own a Nasdaq-100 fund such as QQQ, you may already hold some SPCX, so check before adding.
- SpaceX pays no dividend, so all returns come from the share price.

**Uncertainties:**
- **From earnings-call coverage, not the press release:** the Q1 2027 guide, the run-rate targets, the Q4 margin comment and the consensus comparison.
- **Reported but not confirmed:** "Customer B = Anthropic" is not confirmed by SpaceX, and Anthropic's commitment of up to $120.5B comes from press reports on its registration statement.
- **Secondary sources:** lock-up sizes come from calendars that cite the prospectus.
- **Provider differences:** consensus figures differ by provider.
- **Share count:** it may include about 1.33B unvested Musk performance shares, which are excluded from diluted EPS. Without them, market cap is closer to $1.8T.
- **My own estimates:** the scenario numbers and the Valor interest rate.
